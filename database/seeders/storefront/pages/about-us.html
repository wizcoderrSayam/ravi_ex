About Us

Amritsar da andaaz

Ravi Exclusive sits in Katra Jaimal Singh, one of Amritsar's busiest clothing bazaars. We bring that bazaar energy home: bright Punjabi suits for Lohri and Visakhi, karva chauth lehengas, sherwanis and pagg for the groom, and festive pieces for every rasam.

For the whole parivaar

From bridal lehengas and Punjabi suits to sherwanis, kurta pyjamas, kids' festive wear, safas and juttis, we hand-pick designer ethnic and occasion wear for everyone in the family.

The Punjabi wedding edit

From the mehndi night to the doli, we dress the bride, the groom and the whole baraat: party lehengas and anarkalis for mehndi and sangeet, Punjabi suits for shagan and chunni, bridal lehengas for the bride, and sherwanis, safas and juttis for the groom.

Come to our store for personal styling, trials and fittings for the whole family.
