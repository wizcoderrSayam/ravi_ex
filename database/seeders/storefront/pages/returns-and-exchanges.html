Returns & Exchanges

Size exchanges

If something does not fit, contact us within 7 days of delivery for a size exchange, subject to stock. Items must be unworn, unwashed and returned with all tags and original packaging.

Returns

Returns are accepted within 7 days of delivery for items that arrive damaged or different from what you ordered. Please share photos of the item and packaging on WhatsApp so we can resolve it quickly.

Items we cannot take back

  • Outfits that have been altered or custom-fitted
  • Bridal lehengas and made-to-order pieces
  • Jewellery and accessories, for hygiene reasons
  • Items bought on final sale

Refunds

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method within 7–10 working days of us receiving the item. Cash on delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer.
