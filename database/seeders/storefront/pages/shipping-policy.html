Shipping Policy

Shipping charges

Orders above ₹999 ship free within India. A flat shipping charge of ₹49 applies to smaller orders.

Dispatch

Ready-to-ship orders are dispatched from our Amritsar store within 2–4 working days. Outfits that need alterations or fitting changes are dispatched once the alterations are done; we will confirm the timeline with you on WhatsApp.

Delivery

We ship across India through our courier partners. Delivery usually takes 3–7 working days after dispatch, depending on your location. You will receive tracking details once your order is shipped.

Cash on delivery

Cash on delivery is available on eligible orders and pin codes.
