Privacy Policy

What we collect

When you place an order or contact us, we collect your name, phone number, email address, and billing and shipping addresses. Payments are processed by our payment partner; we do not store your card or bank details.

How we use it

  • To process, ship and support your orders
  • To send order, payment and shipping updates by email, SMS or WhatsApp
  • To answer your questions and requests
  • To send offers and new arrivals, only if you subscribe; you can unsubscribe at any time

Who we share it with

We share only what is needed with our payment gateway and courier partners to complete your order. We do not sell your personal information.
